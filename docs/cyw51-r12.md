## Motion and loading
Required images are decoded and all required font faces are loaded before transition entry. Preparing/retry feedback is outside the mask. No reload, fixed black hold, network await or root reconstruction occurs in a normal transition.

One state machine overlaps document movement with a 160 ms fade to black, performs a synchronous state swap, then fades in for 200 ms. Target total is 360 ms. On reveal, paper and text already occupy their final pose. Pick/put reuse root and paper nodes; enrollment retains the root and removes the submitted paper children only at the opaque swap. Opening is an interactive terminal state, not a busy loading state.
